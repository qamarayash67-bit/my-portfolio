I'm Khadejha Ayyash — a graphic designer and digital marketing specialist based in Amman, Jordan. My work spans brand identity, UI/UX design, social media strategy, product photography, and motion graphics. I engineer experiences that connect brands with the people who matter most to them.

Every project I take on is built from a foundation of intent, precision, and an obsessive eye for detail — because the difference between good design and great design lives in the details.

Social Media Management

Print & Layout Design

Brand Identity Systems

07
Motion Graphics Animation
