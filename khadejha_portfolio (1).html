About Me

I'm Khadejha Omar Salim Ayyash — a graphic designer, photographer, and founder based in Amman, Jordan. My work lives at the intersection of visual identity, digital experience, and brand storytelling.

I graduated in Graphic Design (2026) and have spent the past 1–2 years building brands, designing websites, crafting print materials, and directing visual campaigns for clients across different industries.

I believe great design isn't just beautiful — it speaks, connects, and leaves a mark. Whether it's a logo, a website, or a product shot, I approach every project with intention and craft.
